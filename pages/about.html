NEET Preparation

Suhas Gore is an enthusiastic physics lecturer with 7 years of teaching expertise, primarily focused on preparing students for competitive entrance exams such as JEE, NEET, and CET.

He has a strong academic background in nuclear physics, having studied at the University of Mysore, where he earned recognition for his academic achievements. Over the years, Suhas has developed a deep understanding of the challenges students face while preparing for these exams and has tailored his teaching methods to address these needs effectively.

To further assist students in their preparation, Suhas created this website to share the study material he has carefully crafted. The website serves as a valuable resource for students, providing them with access to high-quality content and expert guidance that reflects his years of experience in the field of physics education.
