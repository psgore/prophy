JEE Advanced 2025 Syllabus CHEMISTRY

General topics

Concept of atoms and molecules; Dalton's atomic theory; Mole concept; Chemical formulae; Balanced chemical equations; Calculations (based on mole concept and stoichiometry) involving common oxidation-reduction, neutralisation, and displacement reactions; Concentration in terms of mole fraction, molarity, molality and normality.

States of Matter: Gases and Liquids

Gas laws and ideal gas equation, absolute scale of temperature; Deviation from ideality, van der Waals equation; Kinetic theory of gases, average, root mean square and most probable velocities and their relation with temperature; Law of partial pressures; Diffusion of gases. Intermolecular interactions: types, distance dependence, and their effect on properties; Liquids: vapour pressure, surface tension, viscosity.

Atomic Structure

Bohr model, spectrum of hydrogen atom; Wave-particle duality, de Broglie hypothesis; Uncertainty principle; Qualitative quantum mechanical picture of hydrogen atom: Energies, quantum numbers, wave function, probability density (qualitative), orbitals; Shapes of s, p and d orbitals; Aufbau principle; Pauli exclusion principle and Hund's rule; Electronic configuration of multi-electron atoms; Elements of periodic table and their chemical properties.

Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure

Ionic bond, covalent bond, coordinate bond, bond parameters, bond order, bond length, bond angle, bond enthalpy, resonance, polarity of bonds, dipole moment, VSEPR model (for the prediction of shapes of simple molecules), valence bond theory (hybridization involving s, p and d orbitals only), molecular orbital theory (homonuclear diatomic molecules only), Hydrogen bonding.

Chemical Thermodynamics

Fundamental concepts of thermodynamics: system, surroundings, state, state functions, path, process, types of processes (reversible and irreversible, isothermal, adiabatic, isobaric, isochoric), internal energy, work, heat, enthalpy, Hess's law, enthalpy of reactions, enthalpy of formation, enthalpy of combustion, enthalpy of fusion, enthalpy of vaporisation, enthalpy of sublimation, enthalpy of solution, first law of thermodynamics and its applications to ideal gases and simple processes, second law of thermodynamics, Gibbs energy change for spontaneous and non-spontaneous processes, equilibrium constant, entropy, third law of thermodynamics.

Chemical Equilibrium

Equilibrium in physical processes and chemical processes, law of chemical equilibrium, equilibrium constant and its relationship with Gibbs energy change, Le Chatelier's principle, factors affecting equilibrium, ionic equilibrium, ionization of acids and bases, weak and strong electrolytes, degree of ionization, pH concept, buffer solutions, solubility equilibria, common ion effect, hydrolysis of salts.

Solutions

Methods of expressing concentration of solutions (molality, molarity, mole fraction, percentage by mass and volume), colligative properties and relative molecular masses (determination of molecular masses using colligative properties), van't Hoff factor, abnormal molecular masses.

Electrochemistry

Redox reactions, electrochemical cells and electrode potentials, Nernst equation and its applications, electrochemical series, conductance in electrolytic solutions, molar conductivity and its variation with concentration, Kohlrausch's law, electrolysis and Faraday's laws of electrolysis, batteries and fuel cells.

Chemical Kinetics

Rate of chemical reaction, factors affecting rate of reaction (concentration, temperature, catalyst), order and molecularity of a reaction, rate law and rate constant, integrated rate equations for zero and first order reactions, half-life, effect of temperature on rate of reaction (Arrhenius equation), collision theory of chemical reactions.

Surface Chemistry

Adsorption (physisorption and chemisorption), factors affecting adsorption, catalysis (homogeneous and heterogeneous), enzymes, colloids (preparation and properties), emulsions, Tyndall effect, Brownian movement, electrophoresis, coagulation.

s-Block Elements

Alkali and alkaline earth metals: Group 1 and Group 2 elements, their electronic configuration, occurrence, anomalous behaviour, diagonal relationship, properties, compounds (oxides, hydroxides, carbonates, bicarbonates, halides), uses.

p-Block Elements

Group 13 to Group 18 elements: General introduction, electronic configuration, occurrence, oxidation states, trends in physical and chemical properties, important compounds, their preparation and properties, interhalogen compounds, noble gases, compounds of xenon.

d- and f-Block Elements

General introduction, electronic configuration, occurrence and characteristics of transition metals, general trends in properties (ionization enthalpy, oxidation states, atomic radii, colour, catalytic behaviour, magnetic properties, formation of coloured ions, interstitial compounds, alloy formation), preparation and properties of important compounds (K2Cr2O7 and KMnO4), lanthanides and actinides, inner transition elements.

Coordination Compounds

Coordination compounds (Werner's theory, VBT, CFT), isomerism (structural and stereoisomerism), bonding in coordination compounds, applications of coordination compounds.

Environmental Chemistry

Environmental pollution (air, water and soil), atmospheric pollution, tropospheric and stratospheric pollution, water pollution, soil pollution, strategies to control environmental pollution, green chemistry.

Practical Chemistry

Detection of extra elements (N, S, halogens) in organic compounds, detection of the following functional groups: hydroxyl (alcoholic and phenolic), carbonyl (aldehyde and ketone), carboxyl, amino and nitro, chemical methods of separation of components of a mixture (two components), qualitative analysis of salts (Group I to V cations and Group I to VI anions), quantitative analysis (estimation of elements and functional groups), preparation of laboratory reagents.
